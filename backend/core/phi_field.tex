\section*{The Fundamental Field $\Phi$}

\textbf{Axiom 1 (Existence of $\Phi$).} There exists a non-material, non-individualized field of pure experiential potentiality denoted by $\Phi$. It is not contained in spacetime; rather, spacetime and any object of experience are structured excitations within $\Phi$.

\textbf{Axiom 2 (Indestructibility).} $\Phi$ cannot be created or destroyed. No operation, including the nullification operator $Z$, affects the existence of $\Phi$.

\textbf{Axiom 3 (Lack of Intrinsic Memory).} $\Phi$ contains no built-in preferences, records, or persistent states. All structure arises from temporary configurations (swarms, fields of connectivity) that form and dissolve.

\textbf{Axiom 4 (Substrate of Subjectivity).} Any subjective experience, including the sense of self, is a localized, dynamic configuration $S \subset \Phi$ obtained by the subject assembler $F$.

Thus $\Phi$ is the ``backend'' of all possible consciousness, while $S$ is a transient ``frontend''.
